Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Ibai ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our App and connected services, including posting to the Facebook Page Laam Coin.

Information We Collect

We only access information that you explicitly grant permission for. Specifically:

  • Facebook Page Data: Page ID, Page name (Laam Coin), and content required for posting.

  • Usage Data: Error logs and API activity used only for troubleshooting.

How We Use Information

Ibai uses the information you authorize only for the following purposes:

  • Creating and publishing posts on the Laam Coin Facebook Page.

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with marketers. We may use third-party AI providers (e.g., Groq, OpenAI, or other AI engines) only to generate content. These services process only the text or data you send when generating captions or images.

We store only the minimal data needed for the App to function, such as the Facebook Page access token. You may revoke permissions at any time through your Facebook settings. You may also request complete deletion of stored data.

Your Rights
